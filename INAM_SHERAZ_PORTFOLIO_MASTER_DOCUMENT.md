### 01 · MOODIYAN TON AGGE (موڈھیاں توں اگے)
**Category**: Degree Thesis & Spatial Installation · 2026  
**Medium**: 40-Page Charcoal Diary, 3-Channel Projection, 9 Recorded Audio Transcripts, Cloned AI Voice Agent, 182 Photographs  
**Core Palette**: Warm Paper (`#E8E2D4`), Charcoal Black (`#0C0B0A`), Deep Rust/Amber  

#### Card Synopsis
> Punjabi for *beyond the shoulders*. About who in a family actually pays for everyone else's better life. I recorded my parents separately and they reached for the same image without knowing it.

#### Detailed Case Study
- **The Concept**: Every generation makes sacrifices, but usually one specific person carries the cost so the next generation can have a better life. In 2007, Inam's father left Pakistan on a 10-day visa aiming for England and ended up in Germany. Inam's mother stayed behind in Pakistan and raised three young sons on her own (Inam was 5 years old).
- **The Name**: Derived from the father's testimony: *"A mother lifts a child into her lap so the child sees as far as she does. A father puts the child on his shoulders so the child sees further than he ever will."*
- **The Unplanned Finding (Convergence)**:
  - Both parents were recorded separately without either hearing what the other said.
  - The Father described planting: *"Every parent puts a seed in the ground and doesn't water it with water — they water it with their own blood."*
  - The Mother described harvest: *"The next generation only sees the crop already standing there; what they don't see is the blood and tears that went into the ground it grew out of."*
- **Installation Components**:
  1. *40-Page Illustrated Diary*: Hand-drawn charcoal scenes documenting the memory and journey.
  2. *Lahoo Naal Sinjeya Boota (3 Projections)*: Three synchronized video loops of a tree at three stages of growth projected on 3 physical boxes where the camera perspective never moves for 20 years and only the ground changes.
  3. *AI Cloned Voice Agent*: Trained on the complete transcripts; speaks in Inam's cloned voice and plays the original raw parental recordings rather than impersonating them.
  4. *Exhibition Room*: Wall of 182 family photographs with an ambient background soundtrack built from their actual spoken voices.

### 02 · DLEA AWARDS 2025
**Category**: Freelance Commercial Motion Package · Dubizzle Labs · 2025  
**Medium**: 4K Screen Graphics, 3D Particle Systems, Ambient Holding Loops  
**Core Palette**: Crimson Red, Gold, Silver, Pure Black (`#000000`)  

#### Card Synopsis
> A technology company throws itself an annual awards night. I built everything that moved on the screens — the opening, and the four loops that hold the room between segments.

#### Detailed Case Study
- **The Problem**: Event screen motion has two conflicting jobs: announcing major moments with high energy, and holding the room ambiently during stage transitions without demanding constant attention.
- **The Execution**:
  - *Announcing*: 3-stage identity reveal — an orchestral opening sting with red bokeh converging into the logo, followed by a gold and silver prestige mark holding the year.
  - *The 19-Second Hold*: A steady, calm hold without a reveal loop that stays on screen while awardees walk to the stage, preventing visual fatigue.
  - *Ambient Holding Loops*: 4 custom-scored motion visual loops (Particle Fields with true z-depth, Expanding Optical Tunnel Squares, Kinetic Line Waves, and Fluid Marble Drift). Designed for the physical scale of a banquet hall rather than a small social reel.

### 03 · THE KING'S HAND (WHEN POWER DEALS CRIME)
**Category**: Self-Initiated Advocacy Design · Proposed for Justice Project Pakistan · 2025  
**Medium**: Screenprinted Playing Card Deck, Interactive Prototype, Motion Loop  
**Core Palette**: Olive Green, Aged Cardstock Cream, Muted Slate Grey, Black  

#### Card Synopsis
> A playing card where the King is a police officer and the Jester is the man he's paying. Four suits, four real cases. Forced to play, framed to fall.

#### Detailed Case Study
- **The Concept**: Crime is often portrayed as street-level deviance, but what happens when it originates from within the uniform? Uses the metaphor of a playing card deck (hierarchy, strategy, expendable pieces).
- **Visual Design**:
  - Court card layout: The King is a decorated, masked police officer; the Jester is a downtrodden civilian taking an unasked-for payment.
  - Kinetic Loop: The diamonds between their hands flow *upward* toward the King, revealing that the officer is extorting the subordinate.
- **The 4 Documented Suits**:
  - Spades: *The King's Kill List*
  - Hearts: *The Family That Folded*
  - Clubs: *The Silenced Jokers*
  - Diamonds: *Burnt for a Price*
- **Tactile Delivery**: Mid-century flat vector aesthetic on aged cardstock. Reads as an attractive collectible object before the grim systemic reality sets in.

### 04 · LIMINAL
**Category**: Mini-Thesis & Mindfulness Publication · Late 2025  
**Medium**: Risograph Folded Manual, Projection Mapping on Print, Ambient Soundscape  
**Core Palette**: High-Contrast Monochrome (Pure Black & Pure White)  

#### Card Synopsis
> Three states — Settle, Breathe, Pause. Not a picture of stillness. An attempt to slow down the act of looking itself.

#### Detailed Case Study
- **The Concept**: Modern attention is endlessly fragmented and solicited. Stillness does not come from isolating oneself from the world, but from engaging with objects that ask nothing from you.
- **The Three Visual States**:
  1. *Settle (Water)*: Movement that exists without demanding cognitive resolution.
  2. *Breathe (Expansion & Contraction)*: An endless breathing loop projected directly onto the printed poster, turning static paper into a living time-holder.
  3. *Pause (Isoline Sphere)*: A 3D illusion formed purely through line density variations with no single focal anchor, allowing the viewer's eye to rest.
- **Artifact**: *Manual for Inner Silence* — an accordion-folded publication using halftone dot fields and concentric line geometry.

### 05 · SCENTS BY AMMAN
**Category**: Brand Identity & Product Packaging · Late 2025  
**Medium**: Fluted Glass Bottle Packaging, Editorial Scent Profile Cards, Anime Convention Pop-Up System  
**Core Palette**: Warm Ivory, Espresso Dark Brown, Sage Green, Muted Gold  

#### Card Synopsis
> A Pakistani fragrance house built on memory instead of price. Everyone else asks what perfume you like. This one asks what you remember.

#### Detailed Case Study
- **The Challenge**: The local fragrance market is polarized between cheap dupe impression counters and faux-luxury gold-embossed boxes. Amman's perfumes are built around nostalgic Pakistani scents (rain on Lahore soil, grandma's dressing table).
- **The Identity**:
  - *Packaging*: Heavy 50ml fluted glass bottles with matte black closures, minimal typography, and 10ml formula reference cards.
  - *Mark*: Organic botanical emblem intertwining leaf and rising smoke silhouettes.
- **The Dual-Tone Adaptation**: When the brand booked a stall at a comic/anime convention, created a bold, character-driven standee and sticker campaign that spoke the subculture's visual language while preserving the parent brand essence.

### 09 · CAT ILLUSTRATIONS (CHILDREN'S APPERCEPTION TEST)
**Category**: Clinical Psychology Research Commission · 2025  
**Medium**: Narrative Digital Line Illustrations, Cultural Transposition  

#### Case Study
- **The Brief**: Master's researcher in psychology needed the 1949 American Children's Apperception Test (CAT) redrawn for Pakistani children.
- **The Problem**: Children in Pakistan spent excessive cognitive effort attempting to decode alien 1950s Western furniture, appliances, and clothing rather than responding to the underlying emotional animal scenarios.
- **The Outcome**: Redrew the situational animal interactions while preserving exact postures and emotional dynamics, transposing all domestic environments into authentic local Pakistani living rooms, verandas, and furnishings.
